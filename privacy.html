Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy describes how VICEPULSE Autopilot ("the app"), a private tool operated by the owner of the VICEPULSE YouTube channel, handles data.

Who uses the app

The app is used only by the VICEPULSE channel owner to manage their own YouTube channel. It has no other users.

Data accessed

With the owner's authorization, the app uses Google OAuth and the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set thumbnails, schedule publication and add videos to playlists on the VICEPULSE channel. It requests the scopes youtube.upload and youtube.

How data is stored

The OAuth refresh token is stored only on the owner's own computer, in a file readable only by that user. Video files, titles and descriptions are created locally. The app has no server and no database, and does not collect, sell or share any personal data with third parties.

Use of Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. See also the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token file also removes the app's access.

Contact

Questions: through the contact details on the VICEPULSE YouTube channel.
